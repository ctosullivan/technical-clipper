# Standards body ratifies date format update

By P. Correspondent · Updated 3 September 2026

The committee approved the amendment after a two-year review, adding clarifications rather than new syntax.

Implementers said the change would have little effect on existing software, because the affected notation was already widely supported.

> “This is housekeeping, and that is a good thing,” one working-group member said.

## What changes

- The end-of-day instant may again be written as `24:00:00`.
- Several definitions were reworded to remove ambiguity.
